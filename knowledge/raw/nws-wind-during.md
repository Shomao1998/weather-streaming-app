# High Wind — Protective Actions

Source: National Weather Service (NOAA), "Wind Safety — During".
Retrieved 2026-08-05. US federal government work, public domain.

## Indoors

- Immediately go inside a sturdy building; move to an interior room.
- If you are in a mobile home, move to a sturdy building before the winds pick up.
- Listen to local news or NOAA Weather Radio for updates.

## Outdoors

- Take shelter in your car if no sturdy building is nearby.
- Avoid trees, power lines, and the side of the road.
- Find a place that will block blowing or falling debris.
- Do not approach power lines lying on the ground; they may be live.

## Driving

- Move your car to a location where it is less likely to be hit by falling trees or power lines.
- When driving near a building, hold the steering wheel with both hands and slow down.
- Keep a distance from high profile vehicles such as trucks and buses; strong gusts can flip them.

## Blowing dust

- Get indoors, close all windows and turn off air conditioning units.
- If driving, pull the vehicle off the pavement as far as possible, stop, and turn off the lights.
