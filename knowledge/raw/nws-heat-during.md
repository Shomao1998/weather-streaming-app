# Extreme Heat — Protective Actions

Source: National Weather Service (NOAA), "Heat Safety Tips and Resources — During".
Retrieved 2026-08-05. US federal government work, public domain.

## Hydration

- Drink plenty of water, non-alcoholic and decaffeinated fluids, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Avoid salt tablets unless prescribed by a physician.

## Timing and activity

- Reduce, eliminate or reschedule strenuous activities until the coolest time of the day.
- Limit children's outdoor play to the cooler times of day.

## Clothing

- Wear lightweight, loose fitting, light-coloured clothing to reflect heat and sunlight.

## Staying cool and shelter

- Use air conditioning, or spend time in air-conditioned public spaces such as malls and libraries.
- Use portable fans to exhaust hot air or draw in cooler air.
- Do not direct a portable fan toward yourself when the room temperature is above 90F (32C).
- Take a cool bath or shower.

## People at higher risk

- Check on older, sick, or frail people who may need help responding to the heat.
- Never leave children or pets unattended in a vehicle, even briefly.
