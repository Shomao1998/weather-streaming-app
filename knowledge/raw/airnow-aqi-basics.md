# Air Quality Index — Categories and Health Meaning

Source: AirNow (US EPA and partners), "AQI Basics".
Retrieved 2026-08-05. US federal government work, public domain.

## Categories

- AQI 0 to 50, Good: air quality is satisfactory, and air pollution poses little or no risk.
- AQI 51 to 100, Moderate: air quality is acceptable. There may be a risk for some people, particularly those who are unusually sensitive to air pollution.
- AQI 101 to 150, Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups: members of sensitive groups may experience health effects. The general public is less likely to be affected.
- AQI 151 to 200, Unhealthy: some members of the general public may experience health effects; members of sensitive groups may experience more serious health effects.
- AQI 201 to 300, Very Unhealthy: health alert. The risk of health effects is increased for everyone.
- AQI 301 and above, Hazardous: health warning of emergency conditions. Everyone is more likely to be affected.

## Interpreting the value

- The higher the AQI value, the greater the level of pollution and the greater the health concern.
- Values at or below 100 are generally considered satisfactory.
- Values above 100 indicate increasingly unhealthy conditions, first for sensitive groups and then for everyone.
