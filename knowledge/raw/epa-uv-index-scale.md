# UV Index Scale — Protective Actions by Category

Source: United States Environmental Protection Agency, "UV Index Scale".
Retrieved 2026-08-05. US federal government work, public domain.

## UV Index 1 to 2 — Low

- No protection needed. You can safely stay outside using minimal sun protection.

## UV Index 3 to 7 — Moderate to High

- Protection needed. Seek shade during late morning through mid-afternoon.
- Apply broad-spectrum SPF-15 or higher sunscreen on exposed skin.
- Wear protective clothing, a wide-brimmed hat, and sunglasses.

## UV Index 8 and above — Very High to Extreme

- Extra protection needed. Be careful outside, especially during late morning through mid-afternoon.
- When your shadow is shorter than you are, seek shade.
- Wear protective clothing, a wide-brimmed hat, and sunglasses.
- Generously apply a minimum of SPF-15 broad-spectrum sunscreen on exposed skin.
