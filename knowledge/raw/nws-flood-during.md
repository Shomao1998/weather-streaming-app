# Heavy Rain and Flooding — Protective Actions

Source: National Weather Service (NOAA), "Flood Safety — During".
Retrieved 2026-08-05. US federal government work, public domain.

## Stay informed and move to safety

- Listen to radio and television, including NOAA Weather Radio if possible.
- If you live in a flood prone area or are camping in a low lying area, get to higher ground immediately.
- If told to evacuate, do so immediately.

## Driving

- Turn Around, Don't Drown. Do not drive into flooded roadways or around barricades.
- 12 inches of water can float a car or small SUV; 18 inches can carry away large vehicles.
- A vehicle caught in swiftly moving water can be swept away in seconds.

## On foot

- Do not walk through flood waters. Six inches of moving water can knock you off your feet.
- If trapped by moving water, move to the highest possible point and call emergency services if possible.

## Electrical hazards

- Avoid basements or rooms where water covers electrical outlets or cords are submerged.
- Leave immediately if you see sparks or hear buzzing, crackling, snapping or popping noises.
- Stay out of water that may have electricity in it.
